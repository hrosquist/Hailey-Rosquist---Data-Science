ABOUT ME

Data, Geography & Curiosity

Hi, I’m Hailey! I’m a Data Science graduate from the University of Oregon with a background in computer science, geography, GIS, and spatial analysis.

I’m especially interested in using data to understand real-world patterns and turning complex information into something clear, visual, and meaningful.

MY STORY

How I Found Data Science

My academic path brought together several interests that I have always enjoyed: problem-solving, technology, geography, and visual communication.

Through studying data science, I learned how to work with large datasets, analyze patterns, build statistical models, and use programming to answer complex questions. At the same time, my geography and GIS coursework showed me how powerful data becomes when it is connected to location and space.

That combination is what interests me most. I enjoy working on projects where I can take raw data, explore what it is telling me, understand the geographic context, and communicate the results through maps, graphics, or visual stories.
